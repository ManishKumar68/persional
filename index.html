About Manish Kumar

Where should I start?

I am Manish Kumar, a Computer Science Engineering student and full-stack developer building practical, responsive, and real-world applications. Explore my project archive for my completed work or visit the Blog page for regular updates on my learning and training.

I build web applications, backend systems, and database-driven solutions using PHP, Java, JavaScript, React, MySQL, and PostgreSQL. My projects include a school management system, documentation hub, tree monitoring platform, contact manager, and Java practice applications.

Yes. I am open to internships, entry-level software development opportunities, and project collaborations. Visit the Contact page to contact me by email or phone, or connect with me on GitHub and LinkedIn.
